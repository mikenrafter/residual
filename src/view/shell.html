Landscape

how components couple, and which attractors pull on them
pinch or ctrl+scroll to zoom · drag to pan · double-click clears selection

Directly connected entities share a force–component membership edge or a force–attractor assignment. Connected entities are the transitive closure: every entity reachable along those direct relations. With multiple selections, connected entities are the union of their reachable sets. The highlight in the views above (full opacity vs. dimmed) follows a partial-transitive set: a selected attractor lights its forces and every component those forces touch; a selected force lights its own components, its attractor, and sibling forces under that attractor (not those siblings' other components); a selected component lights the attractors of forces that touch it and every force under those attractors (not those forces' other components). Coupling lines light only when both ends are in that set.
